ATTEST GOLF UPDATE 2.4.0

INSTALL FROM YOUR IPHONE
1. Tap the downloaded ZIP in Files to extract it.
2. On the Attest-Golf main page in GitHub, use Add file > Upload files. Select app.js, index.html and styles.css from this extracted folder. Commit changes to replace the existing files.
3. On GitHub open netlify/functions. Upload the updated read-card.js from the matching extracted folder and commit changes.
4. Wait for Netlify's latest deployment to say Published, then refresh the app.
5. More > About and accuracy should show 2.4.0.
Use the same site address and existing Netlify provider settings.

THE WORKFLOW
Before the round: tap Before you play: scorecard key. Review the codes and label your paper rows. The Ready to play button does not start a live round, timer, location tracking or reminders.
During the round: use paper and a pencil only.
After the round: tap Add a finished round, choose the card type, photograph it, check readings, confirm the course and save.

COURSE SCORECARD: KEEP ALL FIVE STATS IN FOUR ROWS
SCORE: total strokes including penalties.
PUTTS: strokes on the green only.
TEE + PEN: H hit, L left, R right, S short, LG long. Add a P per penalty stroke (R P = right and one penalty; S P P = short and two). On a par 3, write a dash and add any penalty Ps.
1ST PUTT: A under 3 ft; B 3-6 ft; C over 6-15 ft; D over 15-30 ft; E over 30 ft.
Estimate the range; no measuring required. Leave first-putt distance empty if you did not putt. Leave anything you did not track empty. Use a separate free course card if there are not enough spare rows for your own tracking.

ATTEST PRINTED CARD
Write score, putts and penalty strokes in their separate rows. Circle tee-shot result and first-putt distance. Detailed cards also have approach, contact and short-putt-miss choices. Older printed cards remain supported.

OTHER IMPROVEMENTS RETAINED
- Short and Long work in manual entry, photo review, stats and exports.
- Clearer, larger printed cards with full words and distance choices.
- Different practice plans for 15, 30, 45 and 60 minutes, with drills that fit the allotted time.
- Visual polish while preserving the existing navigation and saved data format.
- Course-card marking key accessible from onboarding, Home, Print and photo upload.
- Existing numeric-foot course cards still supported, although the recommended paper key uses A-E.

CHECKS
All 14 practice focuses were checked at all four durations. Historical scoring and handicap results were compared with the original app. Course-card routes, the no-live-tracking pre-round action, letter and numeric distances, blanks, uncertainty and scanner access controls were tested. Print layouts were checked on landscape US Letter. Real handwriting recognition still needs a test on your deployed site.
